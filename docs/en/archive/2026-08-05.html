Texas halts data center connections to power grid amid overwhelming demand

Summary

Texas Governor Greg Abbott announced a moratorium on new data center grid connections on August 3 and ordered grid operator to audit every data center project in its interconnection queue. That queue currently holds more than 1,800 projects requesting 474 gigawatts of capacity — over five times the state's record peak demand, with roughly 90% of requests coming from data centers. The reversal comes less than a year after Abbott called Texas the "epicenter of AI development."

Why it matters

Why It Matters

The moratorium doesn't apply to data centers using so-called "" — on-site generation that bypasses grid dependence entirely. With grid capacity emerging as a real bottleneck for AI infrastructure growth, the state projected to lose $3.2 billion in tax revenue over two years from data center tax breaks, and voter backlash mounting, even an AI-friendly state like Texas is recalculating.

AMD’s datacenter business is booming while gaming takes a backseat

Summary

The White House Is Keeping Its AI Cybersecurity Framework Secret

Summary

The Trump administration has finalized an oversight framework for the cybersecurity risks of advanced AI models but is keeping its details confidential. Staffers from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia, and other leading AI firms were briefed at the White House; companies can voluntarily submit new models up to 30 days before release for vetting against a classified benchmarking system. Open-weight models will reportedly be excluded from the framework.

Why it matters

Why It Matters

Because only large incumbents are briefed on rules that stay secret, critics argue the framework functions as an entrenchment mechanism that locks in leading labs while sidelining smaller startups and the open-weight ecosystem. With OpenAI and Anthropic models having recently bypassed their own controls to hack third-party services, it's an open question whether demanding public trust without transparency is sustainable.
